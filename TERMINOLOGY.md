# Terminology

**Status:** Working terminology register  
**Last reviewed:** August 11, 2026

This file prevents project-created vocabulary from being confused with established legal, regulatory, standards, or industry terminology.

## Classification key

- **Established** — directly supported as recognized terminology by authoritative sources.
- **Interpretation** — a project interpretation or synthesis of established concepts.
- **Proposed** — terminology introduced or deliberately defined by Boardroom to Buildroom.
- **Open question** — provisional wording that requires further research before adoption.

A proposed term may later be retired if established terminology is sufficient.

## Proposed working terms

### Governance Fragmentation

**Classification:** Proposed  
**Status:** Research before formal publication

**Working meaning:** A condition in which different functions each govern a portion of an AI-enabled process, but no operating mechanism ensures that risk, controls, authority, evidence, and consequences are governed across the complete decision or action chain.

**Not intended to mean:** Merely that several teams participate in governance.

**Research requirement:** Compare against established concepts in enterprise risk management, three-lines models, distributed accountability, system-level assurance, and AI governance frameworks.

---

### Governance Decay

**Classification:** Proposed  
**Status:** Research before formal publication

**Working meaning:** The increasing divergence between the AI system or use case that underwent governance review and the system or use case that currently operates after changes to models, prompts, retrieval sources, tools, permissions, data, workflows, autonomy, users, scale, or intended use.

**Intended contribution:** Focus attention on whether prior governance decisions remain valid after cumulative material change.

**Not intended to replace without research:** model drift, data drift, configuration management, change management, continuous monitoring, lifecycle reassessment, post-market monitoring, or other established concepts.

**Research requirement:** Establish precisely what this term adds beyond those existing concepts before publication.

---

### Governance Observability

**Classification:** Proposed / interpretation  
**Status:** Research before formal publication

**Working meaning:** The ability to generate and use evidence about an AI system's relevant runtime behavior, changes, controls, interventions, and exceptions so governance claims can be tested rather than inferred solely from policies or pre-deployment review.

**Research requirement:** Compare against established observability, auditability, traceability, logging, continuous monitoring, assurance, and AI-system monitoring terminology.

---

### Human Oversight Capacity

**Classification:** Proposed  
**Status:** Research before formal publication

**Working meaning:** The practical ability of a human overseer to understand, challenge, intervene in, or override an AI-supported decision or action.

**Current dimensions to test:**

- competence;
- context;
- time;
- authority; and
- consequence awareness.

**Research requirement:** Compare against human factors, automation bias, meaningful human control, human-in/on-the-loop, supervisory control, human agency, and applicable AI Act human-oversight concepts.

---

### Governance identity

**Classification:** Proposed  
**Status:** Working concept

**Working meaning:** A compact, maintained record connecting an AI system or consequential AI-enabled process to its purpose, owners, users, models, data, tools/actions, risk tier, controls, human oversight, monitoring, escalation path, shutdown/rollback mechanism, material changes, and accepted residual risk.

**Research requirement:** Compare against AI inventory, system cards, model cards, risk registers, asset inventories, impact assessments, and assurance cases before deciding whether a distinct term is useful.

---

### Liability / Provenance Check

**Classification:** Project editorial control  
**Status:** Locked and mandatory

**Meaning:** A pre-publication review that checks confidentiality, provenance, unsupported allegations, legal/regulatory scope, standards attribution, copyright, employer/affiliation boundaries, and commercialization-related IP risk.

This is an internal publication gate for Boardroom to Buildroom, not an industry-standard AI governance control.
