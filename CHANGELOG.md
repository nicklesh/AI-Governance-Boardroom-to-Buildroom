# Changelog

All substantive changes to the Boardroom to Buildroom framework, methodology, terminology, or public-positioning model should be recorded here.

Minor copy edits, formatting changes, and link maintenance do not require entries unless they alter meaning.

## [0.1] — 2026-08-11

### Added

- Created initial public-repository foundation.
- Defined the project as an early-stage practitioner research and public working framework rather than a finished compliance standard.
- Locked the first 10-post Season 1 thesis / pressure-testing sequence.
- Locked the five-anchor editorial test for every Season 1 post.
- Added an anti-repetition rule so accountability remains the series spine rather than the repeated conclusion.
- Added mandatory Liability / Provenance Check as a publication gate.
- Added evidence classifications: Established, Interpretation, Proposed, and Open Question.
- Added initial proposed terminology entries for Governance Fragmentation, Governance Decay, Governance Observability, Human Oversight Capacity, and governance identity.
- Added foundational official sources from NIST, ISO, the European Commission, and OECD.

### Deliberately not finalized

- No proposed terminology is represented as an accepted standard.
- No Boardroom to Buildroom maturity model, certification scheme, scoring methodology, or commercial product is established at this stage.
- No defense-sector governance model is included in the initial scope.
